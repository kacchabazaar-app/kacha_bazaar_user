# Kacha Bazaar - Quick Grocery Delivery App

Welcome to **Kacha Bazaar**, a modern quick-commerce grocery delivery application designed to deliver fresh groceries and daily essentials straight to your doorstep in minutes.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **User Authentication:** Secure sign-up and login system supporting seamless user onboarding and profile management.
* **Category Navigation:** Browse through a wide range of categories including fresh fruits, vegetables, dairy, snacks, beverages, and household essentials.
* **Smart Cart Management:** Easily add or remove items, update quantities, and view real-time price breakdowns.
* **Order Tracking:** Track your order status in real-time from the moment it is packed until it reaches your doorstep.
* **Push Notifications:** Instant updates, order status alerts, and exclusive promotional offers sent directly to your device.
* **WhatsApp Sharing:** Conveniently share product details, offers, or order updates with friends and family via WhatsApp using the official logo integration.

---

## 🛒 How to Order

1. **Sign In / Register:** Open the app and log into your account using your credentials.
2. **Browse Products:** Explore various categories or use the search bar to find the specific items you need.
3. **Add to Cart:** Select your desired quantities and add the items to your shopping cart.
4. **Checkout:** Review your order summary, select your delivery address, and proceed to payment.
5. **Track Delivery:** Sit back and relax while your groceries are quickly dispatched and delivered to you.
